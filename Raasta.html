About

Why Raasta exists

Someone sitting at home in Mehmoodabad wants to reach Saddar. Which bus goes there? Where should they stand to catch it? Raasta answers both questions in one screen, in plain language.

What Raasta tells you

  • Which buses go to your destination, for example R-1 and R-2.
  • Where to board, where to get off, and whether you need to change buses.
  • How long the trip takes and what the fare is.
  • The main roads and stops each bus uses, and how many minutes pass between one bus and the next.

If your spot is not on a bus road

Drop a pin on the map. Raasta finds the nearest place on a bus road where you can board, shows it with a blue line, and tells you how far to walk. The same works for your drop-off: if the bus does not go all the way, you will see where to get off and how far you walk from there.

Built to be simple

Raasta is designed so that anyone can use it, without any technical knowledge.
